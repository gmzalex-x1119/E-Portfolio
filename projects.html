Chromebook Repair Internship

Day-to-day repair work on the student devices the whole campus depends on — diagnosed, fixed and returned.

  • Replaced screens, keyboards, batteries and charging ports, and swapped failed internal components

Geospatial · Data & Mapping Class project

GIS Disaster Response Project

Built flood and earthquake-risk maps of San Bernardino in ArcGIS Pro, then published them as an interactive StoryMap showing where a community is exposed and how response moves.

  • Cleaned raw addresses, infrastructure and hazard data into usable GIS layers in ArcGIS Pro
  • Mapped flood and earthquake risk alongside roads, shelters and response areas
  • Published the finished analysis as an interactive ArcGIS StoryMap
  • Turned messy source data into something a non-technical audience can read and act on
  • Presented the finished map and what it showed to the class
