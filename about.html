// About me

Built by taking things apart.

CyberTech student, repair-bench regular, and someone who would rather understand a system than guess at it.

Who I am

I'm a CyberTech student at Arroyo Valley High School. Most of what I know started with taking apart old electronics, school laptops, and anything with screws - then working out how to put them back together so they actually run.

That curiosity turned toward cybersecurity: how a system is built, where it breaks, and how someone would try to get past it. I like the defensive side best - patching, logging, and cleaning up the small details nobody notices until something fails.

// How I work

Careful hands. Useful notes.

01 Diagnose first

Find the real cause instead of swapping parts until something starts working.

02 Document the fix

A repair nobody can trace isn't finished. Fault in, fix applied, result out.

03 Verify the result

Ask for help early, then confirm the fix myself rather than assuming it's done.
